HACHI Move Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026. Publisher: Takuni product. Android application: com.hachiwellbeing.move.

HACHI Move works without an account. This version has no advertising, paid purchases, remote analytics, cloud synchronisation or automatic data upload. Firebase and advertising auto-start are disabled; this Android build removes Internet and Billing permissions. A future version that enables online services needs an updated policy and disclosure before release.

Data stored on your device

The app stores choices you make and records you save, such as goals, completed activities, structured exercise feedback, HACHI's journey and memories, notes, sound preferences, training or meal-planning selections, manually entered meal records, and Tarot reflection choices. Some features keep bounded local histories. Data stays in the app's private storage until it expires under the relevant feature or you delete it. No camera, microphone, contacts, location, body sensors or Health Connect access is requested. The app is for adults and does not require a child's account.

Reminders

Optional reminders use local Android notifications. Notification permission is requested when you choose reminders. Notifications do not use a remote push service. Android may reschedule a reminder after restart or a clock/timezone change.

Backup and sharing you choose

You can export a backup through Android's document picker. The backup may contain personal journal, activity or meal data, is not encrypted by HACHI Move, and is saved to the destination you choose, which can be a cloud provider. Protect it and delete copies separately. Importing is a separate explicit action; the new app does not automatically read HACHI's existing private storage.

Sharing a postcard or Tarot card opens Android's Sharesheet. The app creates a temporary image; you choose the receiving app and recipient, or cancel. Generic challenge postcards contain art and a leaf count. Tarot cards contain artwork and general card text, not your private question or notes. Temporary sharing files older than 24 hours are pruned on the next image share; Android may clear cached files earlier. The receiving service has its own policy.

External links and support

Links open a separate browser or other app. Those services handle their own traffic and data. If you contact support by email, the contents and address you deliberately send are handled through the support email service to respond to your request; avoid sending sensitive health information. Store-account and distribution data are handled by Google Play, not by HACHI Move's local journal.

Deletion and security

Use the app's data controls to remove supported categories, or clear HACHI Move's app storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove its private local records. Exported backups and shared copies remain wherever you saved or sent them. The app does not provide a server account to delete. Android's application sandbox protects local storage; do not assume exported files are encrypted. The privacy of your device also depends on its security settings.
